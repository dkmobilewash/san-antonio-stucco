## Recent high-impact changes (merged since last report)

Three PRs merged between 2026-10-26 and today that directly affect ranking and conversion:

1. **Redirect equity fix** — combo-page URLs (`/:service/:location`) now 301 to `/:service`
   instead of `/:location`. This was the root cause of service-page impression collapse:
   80 URLs were funnelling link equity to low-value city pages instead of the service pages
   that rank for commercial-intent queries. Impact cannot be measured until 2–4 weeks of
   GSC data accumulate under the new redirect targets.
2. **Inline estimate forms on every service detail page** — reduces friction for visitors
   who land on a service page from search. Previously the only CTA was a link to `/quote`.
3. **Estimate form gate removed** — the two-step service picker that blocked 92% of form
   starts is gone. Fields show immediately.
4. **Address corrected** to match Google Business Profile (18019 San Pedro Ave).

These four changes are the highest-leverage SEO and conversion moves possible right now.
They need time to propagate through Google's index before further content edits are
warranted.
